# Company Policy Handbook

Full-time employees accrue 20 vacation days per year. Up to 5 unused days carry over.

Sick leave: 10 paid days per year.

Paid parental leave: 12 weeks.

Observed holidays: Christmas, Thanksgiving, New Year, Independence Day, Labor Day, Memorial Day. Holidays falling on weekends roll to nearest weekday.

Remote work: 3 days per week.

Meal reimbursement during travel: $75/day.

Harassment reports go to hotline or conduct@company.com.

Passwords: minimum 12 characters, rotate every 90 days.

Performance reviews: semi-annually in June and December.

Two-factor authentication is mandatory on all systems.
